# Research phase 1: Boston vs NYC (checked 16 Nov 2026)

Status: first pass. Figures come from search-result extracts of the linked pages on 16 Nov 2026. No live, date-specific hotel or flight quote was obtained, so every modelled rate below is **unverified** until quoted.
FX used: £1 = $1.32 (mid-market 1.3211, 16 Nov 2026, exchangerates.org.uk).

## 1. Headline findings
- **Memorial Day is Mon 31 May 2027.** The holiday weekend is 29-31 May, i.e. the wedding weekend, not the bachelor weekend. Holiday pricing hits wedding-week hotels and flights home, not 22-23 May.
- **Boston's real pinch on 22-23 May is commencement season.** Boston College commencement is Mon 24 May (inferred from BC's Senior Week page, not yet on the main commencement page), Harvard is Thu 27 May, MIT 26-28 May.
- **Cost is a wash at Budget and Mid** on current assumptions; NYC is about $250 pp dearer at Premium. The result swings on the real Boston hotel rate.
- **Provisional recommendation: Boston**, on logistics and fit. Subject to a real hotel quote and the groom's own preference.

## 3. Accommodation
Market anchors:
- Average May hotel rate: Boston $390, NYC $372 (Engine, 2024-26 data).
- Boston commencement weekends: $509-699 reported, with sell-outs (source is a rental operator's marketing page, treat with caution).
- Booking.com Boston weekend averages: 3-star $290, 4-star $390, 5-star $805.
- Hotel tax: Boston 14.95%. NYC 14.75% plus a flat nightly fee (sources conflict: $1.50 vs about $3.50; model uses $3.50).
- NYC short-term rentals: Local Law 18 limits legal stays under 30 days to two paying guests with the host present, so a group Airbnb in NYC is not realistic. Hotels only (or New Jersey, not researched).
- Boston rentals: legal but restricted to registered, owner-occupied units; supply is thin. Whole apartments sleeping 10 are advertised for graduation weekends (unverified price).
- Hostel: HI Boston takes bookings of 8+ through group sales; online bookings cap at 9 guests. Rates and deposit terms not published: ask.

Deposit and cancellation terms: not verified for any property. Typical US hotel group blocks (10+ rooms) need a contract and deposit; under that, standard flexible rates usually cancel free until 1-3 days before. Confirm per quote.

## 6. Cost model: per person, excluding flights

Rooms: Budget = group/4 rounded up; Mid and Premium = group/2 rounded up.

Not modelled: the nights between the party and the wedding; the groom's share if the group covers him; a second transfer leg for Boston-based guests going to NYC; payment fees.

## 7. Recommendation and counter-case
**Boston (provisional).**
- Logistics: one return flight, one base, no transfer day, and the groom and local guests need no travel.
- Fit: Boda Borg is the only one of its kind in the US and suits a nerdy, social group; add MIT Museum, Tavern of Tales, Versus and improv.
- Cost: level with NYC at Budget and Mid, cheaper at Premium.
- Risk: commencement hotel squeeze. Book refundable rooms early.

**Case for NYC.**
- The groom lives in Boston, so NYC feels like a trip rather than a night out at home.
- Rooms may be cheaper that weekend because Boston is in commencement season; if Boston 4-stars are above about $548, NYC wins on cost even after the train.
- Deeper bench: Comedy Cellar, Beat The Bomb, several board game cafes and arcade bars within a subway ride.
- More flights and lower floor fares from London; a $20 Night Owl train softens the transfer.
- Weak points: no legal group rental, a half-day lost to the transfer, Boston-based guests pay travel both ways, Comedy Cellar cannot be booked far ahead.
