## 6. Payments and currency
- Goal: one payment option that works for UK and US guests, collected well before May 2027, without Jack absorbing exchange-rate swings.
- Constraint: some US guests may not be able to open UK-based accounts (Monzo, Revolut), so the option must work with a normal card or existing account.
- Findings so far (16 Nov 2026, re-verify before committing):
  - PayPal is the best universal candidate; pooling works for non-PayPal users and only the organiser needs an account. Fees are higher.
  - Card payment links (e.g. SignUpGenius) need no account from payers.
  - Wise is usually cheapest (about 0.5-1.5%) and can hold USD, but everyone needs an account.
  - Revolut Group Bills and Monzo Split suit UK guests only.
  - Splitwise tracks who owes what; settlement is separate.
  - Venmo is a poor fit for UK guests.
  - A true rate lock (forward contract) is a business product; overkill here.
- Practical approach: quote each share in the payer's currency, add a 5-10% buffer, collect early, pay big US costs (deposits) as soon as possible, and hold collected USD.
- To verify: PayPal group-pool fees and limits, card-link fees, refund policy, who covers fees.
